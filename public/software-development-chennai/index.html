The gap between spreadsheets and enterprise software

Many Chennai businesses run on a mix of spreadsheets, paper registers and messaging groups. It works until it does not: information gets duplicated, people disagree about the latest version and nobody has a clear picture.

Large packaged software can be heavy and costly to adapt. Custom software, built in stages around your real workflow, can sit comfortably in between.

FAQ

Software Development in Chennai: common questions

Can you build software in stages?

Yes. We usually begin with the most valuable part, launch it, and extend as the business needs it.

Can you move our spreadsheet data into a new system?

Yes. We plan data import as part of the project so history is not lost.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer care plans covering updates, backups, security, monitoring and small improvements.
