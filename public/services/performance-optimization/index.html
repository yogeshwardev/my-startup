FAQ

Common questions

Why is my website slow?

The usual causes are oversized images, too many scripts or plug-ins, slow hosting and heavy page design. A review shows which apply to you.

Can you guarantee a particular speed score?

We commit to measurable improvement and honest reporting. Scores depend on your platform and content, so we do not guarantee specific numbers.

Will optimisation change how my site looks?

No. The aim is the same site, delivered faster.
