FAQ

Common questions

Will my customers know they are talking to an automated system?

You decide. Many automations are invisible — reminders, confirmations, internal notifications. Where a message is automated we keep it helpful and clear.

Do I need to change my current tools?

Usually not. We connect the tools you already use wherever possible.

What if something goes wrong?

We build in checks and alerts, and offer maintenance so automations keep running reliably.
