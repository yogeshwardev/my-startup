How we handle a fix

You describe what is wrong — in plain words is fine, screenshots help. We reproduce the issue, identify the cause, tell you what needs doing and what it will take, and then fix it. Where relevant we test around the fix so one repair does not create another problem.

  • Tell us the problem
  • We inspect and diagnose
  • You get a clear plan and quote
  • We fix, test and confirm with you

FAQ

Common questions

Can you fix a website you did not build?

Yes. Most of our fixing work is on websites and apps built by someone else. We start with an inspection to understand what is there.

Do I need to rebuild everything?

Often not. We will tell you plainly if a repair is enough or if a rebuild would genuinely be the better investment.

How quickly can you start?

Send us the details and we will respond promptly. Urgent problems affecting sales or customers are prioritised.
