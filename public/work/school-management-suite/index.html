Product Concept Schools

School Management Suite

Attendance, fees, timetables, exams and parent communication in one system.

Interface illustration — Product Concept. Not a screenshot of a live client product.

The Problem

Many schools run on separate registers, spreadsheets and messaging groups. Fee tracking is slow, parents are unsure where to find information and staff spend hours on administration.

The Solution

The concept brings the daily work of a school into one system with separate views for administrators, teachers and parents. Each group sees what they need and nothing more.

Key Features

  • Daily attendance for teachers
  • Fee records, dues and receipts
  • Timetable management
  • Examination and results management
  • Parent portal with notices and progress
  • Role-based access for staff

Outcome

Intended outcome: less paperwork, quicker fee follow-up and clearer communication with parents. This is a concept; it has not been deployed in a real school.

What this demonstrates

  • Role-based design
  • Process-led system planning
  • Parent-friendly communication

About this product concept

This was designed by Benzo to illustrate our approach. It is not presented as a paid client project, and it carries no customer testimonials or performance figures.
